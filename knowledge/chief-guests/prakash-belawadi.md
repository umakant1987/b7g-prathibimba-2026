# Prakash Belawadi — Chief Guest, 1 November 2026

## Summary

Prakash Belawadi is a Bengaluru-based actor, filmmaker, theatre personality, journalist, screenwriter, educator and public speaker. His career spans Kannada and English theatre, Indian cinema, filmmaking, journalism, theatre education and cultural activities. He directed the film *Stumble*, which received the National Film Award for Best Film in English.

## What is his role and connection to B7G Kannada Rajyotsava? When is he coming?

Shri Prakash Belawadi is a **Chief Guest** at B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 and is coming on **1 November 2026**, alongside Shri Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli. The 1 November programme begins at 9 AM at Brigade 7 Gardens.

## What films has he directed?

Prakash Belawadi directed the film *Stumble*, which received the National Film Award for Best Film in English. Details of his other directing work haven't been added to the B7G knowledge base yet.

## What are his theatre achievements?

Prakash Belawadi has made significant contributions to Kannada and English theatre. His theatre work received the **Helpmann Award for Best Actor, Male**, for *Counting and Cracking*. He has also been involved in theatre education and in mentoring young people in theatre and film.

## What awards has he received?

Awards and recognition for Prakash Belawadi in the B7G knowledge base:

- **National Film Award for Best Film in English** — for *Stumble*, which he directed.
- **Helpmann Award for Best Actor, Male** — for *Counting and Cracking*.
- Recognition for his contribution to theatre.

## Background

Prakash Belawadi studied Mechanical Engineering at UVCE, Bengaluru, and has a strong connection with Bengaluru and Karnataka. Alongside theatre and cinema, he has worked as a journalist and media professional, and as an educator and public speaker.

## Why is he relevant to Kannada Rajyotsava?

Prakash Belawadi represents **contemporary Karnataka** — theatre, cinema, performing arts, journalism, storytelling, education and modern cultural expression. Alongside Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli, who represents Karnataka's history and heritage, he reflects the connection between Karnataka's past and present.

Prakash Belawadi has acted in Hindi films such as *Madras Café*, *Airlift* and *Talvar*, but he is much more than a Bollywood actor. He is a Bengaluru-based actor, filmmaker, theatre personality, journalist and educator with significant contributions to Kannada and English theatre, cinema and Karnataka's cultural landscape.
