# Nagabhushana NS — Special Guest, 31 October 2026

## Summary

Nagabhushana NS is a Kannada actor known for his natural comic timing and relatable performances. He is particularly known for playing Vasu in the 2021 Kannada comedy film *Ikkat*, opposite Bhoomi Shetty. He has also worked across Kannada films and digital projects.

Nagabhushana NS is the **Special Guest** for the B7G Kannada Rajyotsava programme on **31 October 2026**. ## Ikkat — is he the hero of Ikkat?

Nagabhushana played the lead role of **Vasu** in *Ikkat*, opposite Bhoomi Shetty, who plays Janvi. The Kannada comedy was directed by Haseen Khan and Esham Khan, produced by Pavan Kumar Studios, and released on Amazon Prime Video on 21 July 2021. It follows a young married couple, Vasu and Janvi, whose relationship is tested during the COVID-era lockdown. The film received generally positive reviews, with critics highlighting the performances of Nagabhushana and Bhoomi Shetty.

## What are his achievements?

Nagabhushana won the **SIIMA Award for Best Debutant Actor – Kannada** for *Ikkat*. The film brought him wider recognition among Kannada audiences.
