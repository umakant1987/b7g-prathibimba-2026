# Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli — Chief Guest, 1 November 2026

## Summary

Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli, also known as **Dharmi Sir**, is a historian, author, civil engineer and digital storyteller. Born and raised in Mysuru and based in Bengaluru, he is best known for **Mysoorina Kathegalu — ಮೈಸೂರಿನ ಕಥೆಗಳು** ("Stories of Mysuru"), through which he shares Karnataka's history, heritage, people and places through Kannada storytelling.

## What is his role and connection to B7G Kannada Rajyotsava? When is he coming?

Shri Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli is a **Chief Guest** at B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 and is coming on **1 November 2026**, alongside Shri Prakash Belawadi. The 1 November programme begins at 9 AM at Brigade 7 Gardens.

## What is Mysoorina Kathegalu?

Mysoorina Kathegalu — ಮೈಸೂರಿನ ಕಥೆಗಳು, meaning "Stories of Mysuru" — is the historical storytelling initiative of Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli. Through Kannada digital storytelling, he shares stories about Mysuru and Karnataka's history, heritage, places, personalities, architecture and culture. His storytelling combines **location + history + Kannada + storytelling + digital media**.

## What kind of history does he document?

Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli documents and shares:

- Karnataka, Mysuru and Bengaluru history
- Palaces, forts and temples
- Historical buildings and architecture
- Kings, dynasties and historical personalities
- Culture, local traditions and heritage
- Forgotten stories and historical places

He uses Kannada digital storytelling to make history accessible to modern audiences.

## What books has he written?

Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli is an author, but the titles of his books haven't been added to the B7G knowledge base yet.

## Professional background

Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli is a civil engineer by profession and spent many years working outside India before returning to Karnataka. His interest in history grew from his childhood in Mysuru and the stories he heard about the city's heritage.

## Why is he relevant to Kannada Rajyotsava?

Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli represents **historical Karnataka** — history, heritage, Kannada storytelling, Mysuru and Karnataka's cultural memory. Alongside Prakash Belawadi, who represents contemporary theatre and cinema, he reflects the connection between Karnataka's past and present.

## Is he the Bollywood actor Dharmendra?

No. B7G's Chief Guest is **Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli**, the historian, author and digital storyteller behind Mysoorina Kathegalu. He is a different person from the Hindi film actor Dharmendra and others named Dharmendra Kumar.
