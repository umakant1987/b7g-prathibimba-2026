# Neernalli Ramakrishna — Chief Guest, 15 November 2026

## Summary

Neernalli Ramakrishna (ನೀರ್ನಳ್ಳಿ ರಾಮಕೃಷ್ಣ) is a veteran Indian actor predominantly associated with Kannada cinema. He is known for his versatile acting, memorable performances in classic Kannada films, and his association with legendary filmmaker Puttanna Kanagal. Born in Neernalli, a village near Sirsi in Uttara Kannada district, Karnataka, he has appeared in more than 200 films across Kannada and other South Indian film industries over several decades.

## What is his role and connection to B7G Kannada Rajyotsava? When is he coming?

Shri Neernalli Ramakrishna is a **Chief Guest** at B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026, alongside Shri Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli, and is coming on **Sunday, 15 November 2026** at Brigade 7 Gardens. The day opens with a guest talk from 10 to 11 AM.

## Where is he from? Early life

- **Native place:** Neernalli, near Sirsi, Uttara Kannada, Karnataka.
- He developed an interest in the performing arts as a child and learned **Yakshagana** under the guidance of **Hosatota Manjunatha Bhagavatha**.
- He took part in theatre and stage performances from a young age.
- According to a biographical account, he became the first BA graduate from his village.

His exact date and year of birth have not been confirmed (sources differ, roughly 1951–1954). If asked, say the exact birth date has not been confirmed.

## What was his first film?

His cinematic journey began with the Kannada film **Babruvahana**, in which he portrayed **Lord Krishna** alongside **Dr. Rajkumar**. This brought him into contact with some of the most respected personalities in Kannada cinema, and he went on to work with prominent actors, directors and filmmakers in leading and supporting roles.

## Which Puttanna Kanagal films did he act in?

Neernalli Ramakrishna worked in several memorable films directed by the legendary Kannada filmmaker **Puttanna Kanagal**, including:

- Ranganayaki
- Maanasa Sarovara
- Amrutha Ghalige
- Padavarahalli Pandavaru

His performances in these films helped establish his identity as a talented actor.

## Which legends has he worked with?

Over his career he shared screen space with legendary actors including **Dr. Rajkumar, Dr. Vishnuvardhan, Ambareesh, Anant Nag, Shankar Nag** and **Jai Jagadish**. His recollections of working with them offer insights into the history and evolution of Kannada cinema.

## Life beyond cinema: farming in Neernalli

He has a strong connection with his hometown. He maintains agricultural land in Neernalli near Sirsi and has an interest in farming, particularly **arecanut cultivation**, and has spoken about balancing his acting career with his agricultural responsibilities and his attachment to his native village.

## Public life

In 2004, he contested the Lok Sabha election from the erstwhile Kanara constituency as a Janata Party candidate. He did not win the election.

## Television and later career

From the 1990s onwards he increasingly appeared in supporting and character roles. He has also been associated with television serials and has continued to appear in selected film projects.

## Recognition and legacy

- He is remembered for his contribution to Kannada cinema, particularly his performances from the era of Puttanna Kanagal and Dr. Rajkumar.
- In 2024, he was selected for the **Nammane Award** presented by Vishwashanti Seva Trust Karnataka.
- In October 2025, a biographical book titled **"Inti Nimma Preetiya Ramakrishna"**, written by Vasudeva Shanbhag, was scheduled for release in Sirsi, along with a documentary about his life journey.
