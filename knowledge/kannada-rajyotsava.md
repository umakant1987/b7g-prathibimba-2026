# Kannada Rajyotsava — ಕನ್ನಡ ರಾಜ್ಯೋತ್ಸವ

## What is Kannada Rajyotsava and why is it celebrated on November 1?

Kannada Rajyotsava is celebrated on **November 1** every year. It commemorates the unification of the Kannada-speaking regions into a single state — the day the state was formed — on 1 November 1956. The state was then called Mysore State and was renamed **Karnataka** in 1973.

## What it celebrates

- Kannada language
- Karnataka culture and heritage
- History
- Literature
- Music, dance and theatre
- Community
- Abhimana (ಅಭಿಮಾನ) — pride in Karnataka

## How to talk about it

- Explain Kannada Rajyotsava in a culturally positive and inclusive way.
- It is a day for everyone living in Karnataka — Kannadigas and friends of Karnataka alike — to enjoy and learn about the state's samskruthi (ಸಂಸ್ಕೃತಿ).
- Avoid political arguments or partisan interpretations.
- This is general knowledge, not B7G event-specific information.
