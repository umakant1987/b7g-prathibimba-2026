# Contribute

Shown only while the website's Contribute section is switched on (VITE_SHOW_CONTRIBUTE=true).

## Can I contribute or donate?

Yes — contributions support the community programmes. Use the Contribute button at the top of the website: the Contribute section has QR codes to scan and a phone number for contribution queries.

## How can businesses or well-wishers support the celebration?

Businesses and well-wishers who would like to support B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 are welcome. Point them to the **Contribute** section of the website, which has QR codes to scan and a phone number for queries.
