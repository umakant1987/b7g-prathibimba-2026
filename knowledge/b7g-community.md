# B7G Community

## About B7G

B7G stands for **Brigade 7 Gardens**, a residential community in Bengaluru.

## The Rajyotsava celebration in the community

The Kannada Rajyotsava celebration is a community-driven event intended to bring residents together. It emphasises:

- Community participation
- Family participation
- Children and youth
- Local talent
- Kannada culture
- Cultural learning
- Heritage
- Inclusiveness
- Togetherness

## Inclusiveness

The event is not intended to exclude residents based on language, ethnicity, region or background. The celebration is an opportunity for everyone to learn about and enjoy Karnataka's culture.
