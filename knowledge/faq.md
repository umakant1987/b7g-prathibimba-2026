# Frequently Asked Questions

## When is B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026?

On 31 October and 1 November 2026, at Brigade 7 Gardens (B7G), Bengaluru.

## When does the event start?

The 31 October evening programme runs from 5 PM to 10 PM, and the 1 November programme begins at 9 AM — both at Brigade 7 Gardens.

## Is the event free to attend?

Yes, entry is free and open to all members of the B7G community and their families.

## Do I need to speak Kannada to attend?

Not at all. The celebration is inclusive and welcoming to residents and families from all backgrounds. It's a great opportunity to learn about and enjoy Karnataka's culture.

## Who are the Chief Guests?

The Chief Guests for B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 on November 1 are Shri Prakash Belawadi and Shri Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli.

Prakash Belawadi is an accomplished actor, filmmaker, theatre personality, journalist and educator.

Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli is a historian, author and digital storyteller best known for Mysoorina Kathegalu, through which he shares stories of Karnataka's history and heritage in Kannada.

On 31 October, Kannada actor Nagabhushana NS joins the celebrations as a special guest.

## Who are the guests across the two days?

B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 features different distinguished guests across the celebrations. Nagabhushana NS is associated with the 31 October programme, while Prakash Belawadi and Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli are the Chief Guests for the 1 November celebration.

## Who is Nagabhushana?

Nagabhushana NS is a Kannada actor known for his natural comic timing and relatable performances. He is particularly known for playing Vasu in the 2021 Kannada comedy film Ikkat, opposite Bhoomi Shetty. He has also worked across Kannada films and digital projects and received the SIIMA Award for Best Debutant Actor – Kannada.

## Is Nagabhushana the hero of Ikkat?

Yes. Nagabhushana plays the lead character Vasu in Ikkat, opposite Bhoomi Shetty, who plays Janvi.

## Is Nagendra the hero of Ikkat?

If you mean the hero of Ikkat, you're referring to Nagabhushana NS. He plays the lead character Vasu, opposite Bhoomi Shetty, and is the special guest at B7G Kannada Rajyotsava on 31 October 2026.

## When is Nagabhushana coming to B7G?

Nagabhushana is scheduled to be part of the B7G Kannada Rajyotsava celebrations on 31 October 2026.

## Why were these Chief Guests chosen?

The two Chief Guests bring two complementary dimensions of Karnataka's cultural identity.

Prakash Belawadi represents contemporary theatre, cinema, performing arts and storytelling, while Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli represents Karnataka's history, heritage and Kannada historical storytelling.

Together, they beautifully represent the connection between Karnataka's past and present — history and contemporary culture, heritage and performing arts, tradition and modern storytelling.

## What is Mysoorina Kathegalu?

Mysoorina Kathegalu, meaning "Stories of Mysuru", is the historical storytelling initiative associated with Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli. Through Kannada digital storytelling, he shares stories about Mysuru and Karnataka's history, heritage, places, personalities, architecture and culture.

## What is Kannada Rajyotsava?

Kannada Rajyotsava is celebrated every year on November 1. It commemorates the unification of the Kannada-speaking regions into one state in 1956, and celebrates Kannada language, Karnataka's culture, history, literature, music, dance and theatre.
