# Frequently Asked Questions

## When is B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026?

On **Sunday, 15 November 2026**, at Brigade 7 Gardens (B7G), Bengaluru. The whole celebration happens on this one day.

## Is the event on 31 October or 1 November?

No. The celebration was earlier planned for 31 October and 1 November, but it has been rescheduled to a single day: Sunday, 15 November 2026.

## When does the event start? What is the schedule?

On Sunday, 15 November 2026 at Brigade 7 Gardens:

- **10:00 – 11:00 AM:** Guest talk
- **After the guest talk:** Inauguration of B7G Santhe (the community market). Santhe stays open until the evening.
- **From 5:30 PM:** Cultural programmes, followed by a sand art performance by Mr. Raghavendra Hegde, a renowned sand artist, and then a jamming session to close the evening.

## Is the event free to attend?

Yes, entry is free and open to all members of the B7G community and their families.

## Do I need to speak Kannada to attend?

Not at all. The celebration is inclusive and welcoming to residents and families from all backgrounds. It's a great opportunity to learn about and enjoy Karnataka's culture.

## Who is the Chief Guest?

The Chief Guest for B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 (15 November) is Shri Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli.

Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli is a historian, author and digital storyteller best known for Mysoorina Kathegalu, through which he shares stories of Karnataka's history and heritage in Kannada.

Kannada actor Nagabhushana NS joins the celebrations as the Special Guest.

## Who are all the guests?

B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 on 15 November welcomes Chief Guest Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli and Special Guest Nagabhushana NS.

## Who is Nagabhushana?

Nagabhushana NS is a Kannada actor known for his natural comic timing and relatable performances. He is particularly known for playing Vasu in the 2021 Kannada comedy film Ikkat, opposite Bhoomi Shetty. He has also worked across Kannada films and digital projects and received the SIIMA Award for Best Debutant Actor – Kannada.

## Why is the Chief Guest relevant to Kannada Rajyotsava?

Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli represents Karnataka's history, heritage and Kannada historical storytelling, through his work on Mysoorina Kathegalu. Together with Special Guest Nagabhushana NS, a face of the new generation of Kannada cinema, the guests reflect the connection between Karnataka's past and present.

## What is Mysoorina Kathegalu?

Mysoorina Kathegalu, meaning "Stories of Mysuru", is the historical storytelling initiative associated with Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli. Through Kannada digital storytelling, he shares stories about Mysuru and Karnataka's history, heritage, places, personalities, architecture and culture.

## What is Kannada Rajyotsava?

Kannada Rajyotsava is celebrated every year on November 1. It commemorates the unification of the Kannada-speaking regions into one state in 1956, and celebrates Kannada language, Karnataka's culture, history, literature, music, dance and theatre.
