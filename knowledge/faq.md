# Frequently Asked Questions

## When is B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026?

On **Sunday, 15 November 2026**, at Brigade 7 Gardens (B7G), Bengaluru. The whole celebration happens on this one day.

## Is the event on 31 October or 1 November?

No. The celebration was earlier planned for 31 October and 1 November, but it has been rescheduled to a single day: Sunday, 15 November 2026.

## When does the event start? What is the schedule?

On Sunday, 15 November 2026 at Brigade 7 Gardens:

**Morning session (8:30 – 11:00 AM)**
- 8:30 AM: Arrival of guests
- 8:45 AM: Breakfast with the Chief Guests
- 9:10 AM: Invocation song by Poornima and lamp lighting ceremony
- 9:20 AM: Welcome address by the President
- 9:30 – 10:30 AM: Samvada, an interaction with the Chief Guests, Shri Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli and Shri Neernalli Ramakrishna
- 10:30 AM: Chief Guests' interaction with the GSS team
- 10:45 AM: Inauguration of B7G Santhe (the community market stays open until the evening)

**Evening session · Cultural Programme (from 4:40 PM)**
- 4:40 – 7:05 PM: Performances by B7G residents and groups — group song, kids dance, flute, group yoga, instrumental, singing, violin, dance dramas, a dance-cum-skit and Naada Geethe
- 7:05 – 8:00 PM: Sand art by Mr. Raghavendra Hegde, a renowned sand artist
- 8:45 – 10:30 PM: Jamming, the last item of the day

Kannada actor Nagabhushana NS is the Chief Guest for the evening. The full running order with item-wise timings is in the Programmes section of the website.

## Is the event free to attend?

Yes, entry is free and open to all members of the B7G community and their families.

## Do I need to speak Kannada to attend?

Not at all. The celebration is inclusive and welcoming to residents and families from all backgrounds. It's a great opportunity to learn about and enjoy Karnataka's culture.

## Who are the Chief Guests?

B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 (15 November) has three Chief Guests: Shri Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli and Shri Neernalli Ramakrishna for the morning programme, and Kannada actor Nagabhushana NS for the evening programme.

Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli is a historian, author and digital storyteller best known for Mysoorina Kathegalu, through which he shares stories of Karnataka's history and heritage in Kannada.

## Who are all the guests?

B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 on 15 November welcomes morning Chief Guests Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli and Neernalli Ramakrishna, and evening Chief Guest Nagabhushana NS.

## Who is Nagabhushana?

Nagabhushana NS is a Kannada actor known for his natural comic timing and relatable performances. He is particularly known for playing Vasu in the 2021 Kannada comedy film Ikkat, opposite Bhoomi Shetty. He has also worked across Kannada films and digital projects and received the SIIMA Award for Best Debutant Actor – Kannada.

## Why are the Chief Guests relevant to Kannada Rajyotsava?

Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli represents Karnataka's history, heritage and Kannada historical storytelling, through his work on Mysoorina Kathegalu. Neernalli Ramakrishna, a veteran of more than 200 films and of classic Kannada cinema with Puttanna Kanagal and Dr. Rajkumar, represents Kannada cinema's rich legacy, with roots in Yakshagana and theatre. Together with evening Chief Guest Nagabhushana NS, a face of the new generation of Kannada cinema, the guests reflect the connection between Karnataka's past and present.

## What is Mysoorina Kathegalu?

Mysoorina Kathegalu, meaning "Stories of Mysuru", is the historical storytelling initiative associated with Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli. Through Kannada digital storytelling, he shares stories about Mysuru and Karnataka's history, heritage, places, personalities, architecture and culture.

## What is Kannada Rajyotsava?

Kannada Rajyotsava is celebrated every year on November 1. It commemorates the unification of the Kannada-speaking regions into one state in 1956, and celebrates Kannada language, Karnataka's culture, history, literature, music, dance and theatre.
