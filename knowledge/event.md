# B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026

## Event identity

- **Event:** B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026
- **Date:** Sunday, 15 November 2026 (one day)
- **Community:** Brigade 7 Gardens (B7G), Bengaluru

## Schedule for 15 November 2026

The whole celebration happens on one day, Sunday 15 November 2026, at Brigade 7 Gardens.

**Morning**
- **10:00 – 11:00 AM:** Guest talk
- **After the guest talk:** Inauguration of B7G Santhe (the community market). Santhe stays open until the evening.

**Evening**
- **From 5:30 PM:** Cultural programmes, followed by a sand art performance by Mr. Raghavendra Hegde, a renowned sand artist, and then a jamming session to close the evening.

## Was the event moved? Is it on 31 October or 1 November?

Yes, it was rescheduled. The celebration was earlier planned for 31 October and 1 November 2026; it now takes place on a single day, **Sunday 15 November 2026**. If anyone asks about 31 October or 1 November (for example because they saw an older poster), tell them the celebration has moved to 15 November 2026. Kannada Rajyotsava itself is still observed on 1 November every year; only the B7G celebration date changed.

## Guests

| Guest | Role |
|---|---|
| Shri Dharmendra Kumar Arenahalli | Chief Guest |
| Shri Neernalli Ramakrishna — veteran Kannada actor | Chief Guest |

All three are guests of the celebration on 15 November 2026. The day opens with a guest talk from 10 to 11 AM.

## What the event celebrates

B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 is a community celebration of:

- Kannada language
- Karnataka culture
- Karnataka history
- Heritage
- Music
- Dance
- Theatre
- Literature
- Local talent

## Spirit of the celebration

The celebration is **inclusive and welcoming**. Its purpose is to celebrate Kannada and Karnataka while welcoming residents and families from all backgrounds. It is an opportunity for everyone to learn about and enjoy Karnataka's samskruthi (ಸಂಸ್ಕೃತಿ) and parampare (ಪರಂಪರೆ).

**Learn. Celebrate. Connect.**

**Namma Kannada. Namma Karnataka. Namma Hemme. ❤️**
