# Dr. Raja Ramanna — Nuclear Scientist and Padma Vibhushan Awardee (ಡಾ. ರಾಜಾ ರಾಮಣ್ಣ — ಅಣು ವಿಜ್ಞಾನಿ, ಪದ್ಮವಿಭೂಷಣ ಪುರಸ್ಕೃತರು)

Featured in the "Other Significant Achievements" card of the website's "What Karnataka Gave India" section. He is not a guest at B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026.

## Summary

Dr. Raja Ramanna (1925–2004), born in **Tumakuru, Karnataka**, was a nuclear physicist who played a central role in building India's nuclear science and energy programme. He led the scientific team behind **India's first nuclear test, Pokhran-I, in 1974**, and received the **Padma Vibhushan in 1975**.

## What did he contribute?

Ramanna worked at the Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC) from its early years and helped build India's capability in nuclear physics and nuclear reactors, which are used for research and for producing electricity. He served as **Director of BARC** and as **Chairman of the Atomic Energy Commission**, guiding the country's nuclear research and nuclear energy programme. In simple words for children: he helped India learn to use the energy hidden inside the atom.

## His most important achievement

He was the scientific leader of **Pokhran-I on 18 May 1974**, India's first nuclear test, which India described as a peaceful nuclear explosion. It showed that Indian scientists could master one of the most advanced fields of science using their own knowledge and skill.

## How did his work benefit India and humanity?

His leadership strengthened India's scientific self-reliance and its strategic capability, and the nuclear programme he helped build supports research and nuclear power in India. He also served the nation as Minister of State for Defence and as a Member of Parliament (Rajya Sabha), and was the first Director of the National Institute of Advanced Studies (NIAS) in Bengaluru. He was also an accomplished pianist who wrote about music, a reminder that science and the arts can live in the same mind.

## Connection to Karnataka

He was born in Tumakuru and is one of Karnataka's most distinguished scientists. In Bengaluru he was the founding Director of the National Institute of Advanced Studies.

## Why we should remember him

Raja Ramanna shows that a scientist from Karnataka could lead India into the nuclear age, with knowledge, discipline and a love for music too.

- **ಪ್ರಮುಖ ಸಾಧನೆ:** 1974ರ ಮೇ 18ರಂದು ನಡೆದ ಭಾರತದ ಮೊದಲ ಅಣು ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ ಪೋಖ್ರಾನ್–1ರ ವೈಜ್ಞಾನಿಕ ನಾಯಕತ್ವ ವಹಿಸಿದ್ದರು. 1975ರಲ್ಲಿ ಪದ್ಮವಿಭೂಷಣ ಗೌರವ ಸಂದಿತು.
- **ಭಾರತಕ್ಕೆ ಲಾಭ:** ಭಾರತದ ವೈಜ್ಞಾನಿಕ ಸ್ವಾವಲಂಬನೆ ಮತ್ತು ವ್ಯೂಹಾತ್ಮಕ ಸಾಮರ್ಥ್ಯವನ್ನು ಬಲಪಡಿಸಿದರು. ರಕ್ಷಣಾ ಖಾತೆಯ ರಾಜ್ಯ ಸಚಿವರಾಗಿ, ರಾಜ್ಯಸಭಾ ಸದಸ್ಯರಾಗಿ ಮತ್ತು ಬೆಂಗಳೂರಿನ ರಾಷ್ಟ್ರೀಯ ಉನ್ನತ ಅಧ್ಯಯನ ಸಂಸ್ಥೆಯ (NIAS) ಮೊದಲ ನಿರ್ದೇಶಕರಾಗಿ ಸೇವೆ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿದರು. ಅವರು ಉತ್ತಮ ಪಿಯಾನೋ ವಾದಕರೂ ಆಗಿದ್ದರು.
