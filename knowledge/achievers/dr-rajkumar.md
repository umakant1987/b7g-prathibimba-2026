# Dr. Rajkumar — Dadasaheb Phalke Awardee (ಡಾ. ರಾಜ್‌ಕುಮಾರ್ — ದಾದಾಸಾಹೇಬ್ ಫಾಲ್ಕೆ ಪ್ರಶಸ್ತಿ ಪುರಸ್ಕೃತರು)

Featured in the "Other Significant Achievements" card of the website's "What Karnataka Gave India" section. He is not a guest at B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026.

## Summary

Dr. Rajkumar (1929–2006), lovingly called **"Annavru"** and honoured with the title **"Nata Saarvabhouma"**, is the greatest icon of Kannada cinema. An actor, singer and theatre artist, he acted in more than 200 Kannada films and received the **Dadasaheb Phalke Award for 1995**, India's highest honour in cinema.

## What did he contribute?

He grew up in theatre, performing with Gubbi Veeranna's famous drama company, and got his first lead film role in *Bedara Kannappa* (1954). Over five decades he played kings, saints, poets, farmers and ordinary people, in films such as *Bhakta Kumbara*, *Mayura*, *Sri Krishnadevaraya*, *Kaviratna Kalidasa* and *Bangarada Manushya*, many of them based on Kannada literature and history. He was also a much-loved **singer**, and won the National Award for Best Male Playback Singer (1993) for the song "Naadamaya Ee Lokavella". In simple words for children: through his films and songs, he brought Kannada stories, language and values into every home.

## His most important achievement

His greatest achievement was making Kannada cinema a voice of **Kannada identity and pride**. His clear, beautiful Kannada, his disciplined life and the values in his films made him a role model for millions. The **Dadasaheb Phalke Award (1995)** recognised his lifetime contribution to Indian cinema.

His other major honours include:

- **Padma Bhushan (1983)**
- **Karnataka Ratna (1992)**, Karnataka's highest civilian honour
- **National Award for Best Male Playback Singer (1993)**, for the song "Naadamaya Ee Lokavella"

## How did his work benefit Karnataka and India?

He popularised the Kannada language, literature and culture across generations, and inspired people to take pride in their mother tongue. In 1982 he lent his voice and presence to the **Gokak movement**, which called for Kannada to be given its rightful place in education in Karnataka, turning it into a people's movement.

## Why does he remain an icon of Kannada pride?

Decades later, his songs are still sung and his films still watched by families across Karnataka. For many Kannadigas, Dr. Rajkumar stands for the best of Kannada: its language, its culture and its values.

## Why we should remember him

Dr. Rajkumar teaches us that art can do more than entertain: it can unite a people and keep a language alive in their hearts.

- **ಯಾರು:** ಡಾ. ರಾಜ್‌ಕುಮಾರ್ (1929–2006), ಪ್ರೀತಿಯ "ಅಣ್ಣಾವ್ರು", "ನಟಸಾರ್ವಭೌಮ". ಕನ್ನಡ ಚಿತ್ರರಂಗದ ಮಹಾನ್ ನಟ, ಗಾಯಕ ಮತ್ತು ರಂಗಕಲಾವಿದ.
- **ಕೊಡುಗೆ:** ಗುಬ್ಬಿ ವೀರಣ್ಣನವರ ನಾಟಕ ಕಂಪನಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ರಂಗಭೂಮಿಯಿಂದ ಬೆಳೆದು, "ಬೇಡರ ಕಣ್ಣಪ್ಪ" (1954) ಚಿತ್ರದ ಮೂಲಕ ನಾಯಕರಾದರು. 200ಕ್ಕೂ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಚಿತ್ರಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ರಾಜರು, ಸಂತರು, ಕವಿಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಜನರ ಪಾತ್ರಗಳನ್ನು ನಿರ್ವಹಿಸಿದರು: "ಭಕ್ತ ಕುಂಬಾರ", "ಮಯೂರ", "ಶ್ರೀ ಕೃಷ್ಣದೇವರಾಯ", "ಕವಿರತ್ನ ಕಾಳಿದಾಸ", "ಬಂಗಾರದ ಮನುಷ್ಯ" ಮುಂತಾದವು. "ನಾದಮಯ ಈ ಲೋಕವೆಲ್ಲಾ" ಹಾಡಿಗೆ ಶ್ರೇಷ್ಠ ಗಾಯಕ ರಾಷ್ಟ್ರಪ್ರಶಸ್ತಿ (1993) ಪಡೆದರು.
- **ಪ್ರಮುಖ ಸಾಧನೆ:** ಕನ್ನಡ ಚಿತ್ರರಂಗವನ್ನು ಕನ್ನಡ ಅಸ್ಮಿತೆ ಮತ್ತು ಅಭಿಮಾನದ ಧ್ವನಿಯನ್ನಾಗಿಸಿದರು. ಭಾರತೀಯ ಚಿತ್ರರಂಗಕ್ಕೆ ನೀಡಿದ ಜೀವಮಾನದ ಕೊಡುಗೆಗಾಗಿ 1995ರ ದಾದಾಸಾಹೇಬ್ ಫಾಲ್ಕೆ ಪ್ರಶಸ್ತಿ ಸಂದಿತು. ಪದ್ಮಭೂಷಣ ಪ್ರಶಸ್ತಿ (1983), ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ ರತ್ನ ಪ್ರಶಸ್ತಿ (1992) ಹಾಗೂ ಶ್ರೇಷ್ಠ ಗಾಯಕ ರಾಷ್ಟ್ರಪ್ರಶಸ್ತಿ (1993) ಗೌರವಗಳೂ ಸಂದಿವೆ.
- **ಕರ್ನಾಟಕಕ್ಕೆ ಲಾಭ:** ಕನ್ನಡ ಭಾಷೆ, ಸಾಹಿತ್ಯ ಮತ್ತು ಸಂಸ್ಕೃತಿಯನ್ನು ತಲೆಮಾರುಗಳಿಗೆ ತಲುಪಿಸಿದರು. 1982ರಲ್ಲಿ ಶಿಕ್ಷಣದಲ್ಲಿ ಕನ್ನಡಕ್ಕೆ ಪ್ರಾಧಾನ್ಯ ಕೋರಿದ ಗೋಕಾಕ್ ಚಳವಳಿಗೆ ಧ್ವನಿಯಾಗಿ ಅದನ್ನು ಜನಚಳವಳಿಯನ್ನಾಗಿಸಿದರು.
- **ಏಕೆ ನೆನಪಿಡಬೇಕು:** ಕಲೆ ಮನರಂಜನೆಗಿಂತ ಹೆಚ್ಚು: ಅದು ಜನರನ್ನು ಒಗ್ಗೂಡಿಸಿ, ಭಾಷೆಯನ್ನು ಹೃದಯಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಜೀವಂತವಾಗಿಡಬಲ್ಲದು ಎಂಬುದನ್ನು ಡಾ. ರಾಜ್‌ಕುಮಾರ್ ಕಲಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ.
