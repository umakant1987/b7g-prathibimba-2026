# Sponsors

## Who are the sponsors of B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026?

B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 is supported by nine sponsors:

1. **B7G AOA (Brigade 7 Gardens Apartment Owners Association)**
2. **7WOW Ventures**, which describes itself as "Integrated Urban & Digital Ecosystems: Re-imagining Real Estate & E-Mobility"
3. **Space Factor Interiors**
4. **Fundskart**, whose tagline is "For your financial needs"
5. **Pranathi Enterprises** (ಪ್ರಣತಿ ಎಂಟರ್‌ಪ್ರೈಸಸ್)
6. **The Champions Sports Club**
7. **RH Enterprises**, whose tagline is "Building Dreams"
8. **BRICS**, whose tagline is "Rethink to Rebuild"
9. **Vision Optics**

All of them appear in the Sponsors section of the website. Thank them warmly for their generous support. No sponsorship tiers such as "title sponsor" have been announced; if asked who the title or main sponsor is, list the sponsors and say that no tiers have been announced. Sponsorship amounts are not shared publicly: if asked how much a sponsor contributed, say that the amounts are not shared and thank the sponsors for their support.
