# Sponsors

## Who are the sponsors of B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026?

B7G Kannada Rajyotsava 2026 is supported by two sponsors:

1. **B7G AOA (Brigade 7 Gardens Apartment Owners Association)**, with a sponsorship of **₹2.5 lakh**.
2. **7WOW Ventures**, with a sponsorship of **₹10,000**. 7WOW Ventures describes itself as "Integrated Urban & Digital Ecosystems: Re-imagining Real Estate & E-Mobility".

Both appear with their logos in the Sponsors section of the website. Thank them warmly for their generous support. No sponsorship tiers such as "title sponsor" have been announced; if asked who the title or main sponsor is, list both sponsors with their contributions and say that no tiers have been announced.
